Important Pricing Policies

Zero Ambiguity • Institutional Trust
verified Formal Project Briefs

Final fees are confirmed only after our strategists complete an in-depth requirements review and issue an itemized, binding Statement of Work (SOW).

verified Platform Ad Budgets

Advertising spend for Google Ads and Meta Ads is paid directly to platforms by the client. We do not markup media spend nor disguise platform billing.

verified Infrastructure & Taxes

Domain purchases, dedicated cloud hosting, third-party premium SaaS API licenses, and statutory GST (18%) are billed transparently as separate line items.

verified Ethical Agency Practice

DEEPVIXO strictly avoids deceptive promises such as guaranteed #1 search rankings or viral traffic. We deploy rigorous, verifiable growth engineering.

verified Monthly Retainer Flexibility

Monthly marketing contracts feature scheduled performance review cadences and clean notice periods without punitive lock-in clauses.
